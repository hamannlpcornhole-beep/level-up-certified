FAQ

Questions? Answers.

What is Level Up Certified?

A short online course that covers everything a cornhole player should know. Rules, etiquette, gear, apps and how to play. Finish it and you get a player card that proves it.

How much is it?

$1. That covers the full course, your player card and your giveaway entries for the year.

How long does it take?

About 35 minutes. Your progress saves, so you can stop and pick back up anytime.

Do I have to renew?

Yes. Certification lasts one year. Renew to stay certified and stay in the giveaways.

My league requires it. How do they check?

Your card has a QR code and a certification ID. Your league scans the code or types the ID on the verify page and gets a yes or no right away.

Whose rules does it teach?

Official ACL rules, explained in plain English. Your league might add house rules on top, so always check with your director.

Is it just for beginners?

It's built so new players can jump right in. But plenty of experienced players pick up something new in the rules and etiquette modules.

Can I use the rest of the site without paying?

Yep. The gear guide, where to play, the training center and the verify page are all free.
