Bags 101

What makes a bag legal.

These are the ACL numbers. Backyard bags can be anything, but if you want to play league or ACL events, this is the spec.

Size and weight

6 inches by 6 inches, 15.5 to 16.5 ounces, and 1.1 to 1.5 inches thick. Anything outside that is a backyard bag.

Resin fill

League bags are filled with plastic resin so they play the same every night. You can't add or take out fill after you buy them.

Two different sides

A slick side that slides farther and a sticky side that stops faster. The sides can be different, but all 4 bags in your set have to match.

The stamp

Approved bags get stamped PRO, COMP, REC or MINI. For ACL events they have to be on the current approved list, stamps from before 2023 don't count, and the Pro division needs PRO.

Bag speed

Slide it or stop it.

Every company rates speed a little differently, but most use a 1 to 10 scale. Low numbers grab, high numbers slide. Drag the slider and watch what happens.

Boards 101

The spec sheet.

Practice on the most regulation board you can get. A board that plays different than league boards builds habits you have to unlearn.

Board size2 ft x 4 ft
Hole6 in across, centered, 9 in from the back edge
Back edge height12 in off the ground
Front edge height2.5 to 3.5 in (ACL)
Thickness1/2 in with backing, or 3/4 in without
Weight15 lb minimum (ACL)
Distance apart27 ft, front edge to front edge
SurfaceSmooth wood with a slick finish

Backyard vs regulation

Backyard sets are usually lighter, thinner and sometimes short of 2x4. Fine for the driveway. If you're practicing for league night, you want a regulation surface and the real 27 feet.

Take care of it

Keep it dry, wipe it with a dry cloth, and don't put anything on the surface. A sticky board makes slides impossible and a wet board warps.
