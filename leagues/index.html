    League FAQ

    Straight answers.

    What does it cost the league?

    Nothing. You share your link, your players pay a dollar each, and your league earns a share of it.

    What do players pay?

    $1, and it's good for a year. They get the full course and a player card.

    How do we check players at the door?

    Open the verify page on your phone and scan the QR code on their card, or type their ID. You get a green or red answer in a second.

    Whose rules does it teach?

    Official ACL rules, in plain English. Your house rules go on top, and the course tells players to check with their director.

    What if a player isn't certified yet?

    They can do it on their phone before games start. Your league decides the policy: required for everyone, required for brackets, or just encouraged.

    How does the revenue share work?

    Every certification that comes through your league link is tracked to your league. Your share is set in your partner agreement, based on your size.

    Do players renew?

    Yes, once a year. They get a reminder before it lapses, and your league keeps earning on renewals.
